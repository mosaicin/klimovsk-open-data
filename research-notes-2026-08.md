## Public procurement and construction history

A local publication identifies the company as the participant and reports the later project cost split; this is an event record, not a finding of wrongdoing. Source: https://www.podolsk.ru/news/n30216.html

## Interpretation boundary

Repeated contractors and a former military site are verifiable facts. They are not by themselves evidence of fraud, organized crime, or a conspiracy. Procurement integrity requires the official notice, protocol, contract, amendments, acceptance acts, payments, and court/audit findings. Any public presentation must distinguish fact, source, and hypothesis.
