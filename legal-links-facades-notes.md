### Implication for legal-link analysis

The evidence currently supports a distinction between: (a) the Podolsk ООО «Триумф» connected to published school procurement references; (b) a separate ООО «Триумф Групп» registered in Klimovsk; and (c) an unresolved identity for ООО «ГРМ». No public source reviewed here establishes ownership of the land at Suvorova Street 2A–2I by any of these contractors. A legal link requires matching INN/OGRN, a formal address history, a documented founder/manager relationship, an official contract, or an official land/title record; similar names and facade appearance are insufficient.
